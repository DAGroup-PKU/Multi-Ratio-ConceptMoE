Abstract

Compute demand changes across conversational turns. Using a large model throughout can waste computation, while switching between independent models may require rebuilding their history states.

We introduce Multi-Ratio ConceptMoE, which jointly supports compression ratios of 2, 4, and 8 within a shared backbone. Lightweight full-token modules reduce compression-independent cost, ratio-specific boundary routers expose distinct compute budgets, and cross-ratio training prepares the model to process different compression granularities in the same context.

Across PTBench and SFTBench, all three fixed modes outperform their compute-matched MoE baselines. On CodeChat and CoSQL, mixed-ratio inference improves the accuracy-compute trade-off while avoiding the history re-prefill overhead accounted for when routing among independent models.

Method: Multi-Ratio ConceptMoE

Only the boundary routers are ratio-specific. The encoder, concept backbone, DeChunk module, and decoder are shared.
